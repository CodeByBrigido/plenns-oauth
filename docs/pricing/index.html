Common questions

Is the 1% on top of Stripe's fee?

Yes. Stripe charges its own processing fee on every payment. It depends on the card and your country, and it's separate from Plenns's 1%.

What happens after the 14 free days?

Add a card to keep your page live. Without one, your subscription pauses and your page stops taking new bookings.

What if I refund a client?

Plenns's 1% comes back to you. Stripe keeps its processing fee, so a refund does cost you that part. We'll show this when you set your cancellation policy.

Can I charge in my own currency?

Yes. Plenns suggests one from your country, and you can change it. Your subscription is billed in that currency when we offer it, and in euros when we don't.

Which countries can use Plenns?

Plenns works in the countries where Stripe lets us take this fee for you, and you'll see yours when you sign up. Brazil isn't one of them: Stripe doesn't let platforms outside Brazil collect this kind of fee from Brazilian businesses.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They book as guests with a name and an email, and pay by card on the same page.
