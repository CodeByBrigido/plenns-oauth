About online booking

Your page stays open all night. A client picks a person, a day and an open time, then pays. The time is held while they do, so nobody else can take it.

  • A landing page on Plenns, or a widget on your own website
  • A calendar that only shows the days and times still open
  • Clients book as guests with their name and email
  • Confirmation emails to the client and to you
  • Several services in one booking, added up for time and price
  • Clients pick a person, or Plenns assigns one who's free
  • Two clients can never take the same time
