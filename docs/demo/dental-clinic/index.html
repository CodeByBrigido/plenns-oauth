Before your visit

  • New patients can start with a dental check-up.
  • Allow an extra ten minutes for the first visit.
  • For a root canal or an extraction, select the treatment once a dentist has assessed the tooth.
  • Bring a list of current medicines and any relevant dental records to a first appointment.
  • Demo payments are simulated. No contact or card details are collected.

Things to know

Can I choose my dentist?
Yes. Choose a service to see the professionals who provide it.
When will I see the price?
After choosing your time, you can review the full price and the 50% deposit option.
Can I change my appointment?
Use the confirmation to select another available date or time and review the 15% fee.
